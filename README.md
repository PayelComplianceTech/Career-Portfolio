COMPLIANCE

This case study documents how I led my team through a successful ISO 9001 audit in September 2026

Recently, my team was selected for an ISO audit - obviously being the leader I had to handle this end to end.

ISO Audit is mainly for quality management (QMS), you see working in healthcare compliance or any sector you will always have an audit structure as you provide services to the client outside of India.
It is extremely important to stay on top every aspect in audits and compliance- no compromising ever!

The department which was audited was QSW (Qualified Social Work) which is one of my team.

The documents we needed ready for this audit are as below:
- Risk Reigster (1 year)
- Quality Assurance Log (1 year)
- Performance Metrics 
- Governance Log 
- Error and Escalations Log (3 years log)
- SOPs (3 years log) 
What are each of these documents and how it should be maintained is explained in this short video
We passed the audit on the first attempt due to proactive documentation and evidence management
Key is to remain on top with these requirements, so you aren't hurdling at seventh hour
